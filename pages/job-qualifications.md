
Job Qualification Rules 

Klaviyo - WW (Klaviyo Worldwide)
Skip if any of the following are true:
- job.description does not contain "Klaviyo".
- job.description emphasizes Shopify development, web design, copywriting, CRO, graphic design, VA, or entry-level tasks over Klaviyo.
- job.description involves developer or engineering tasks (store, site, theme, app) even if Klaviyo is mentioned.
- job is for video editing, paid ad optimization, landing page building, etc.
- clientDetails.categoryName is "agency", "consultancy", "white-label provider", or involves sub-contracting.
- job.description mentions agency or consultancy.
- job.description involves account management for an agency.
- job.duration is "less_than_a_month" and the task is a one-time task under one hour.
- clientDetails.totalReviews < 4.5.
- clientDetails.history of disputes exists.
- proposalsCount > 20.
- job.skills has any of these: Virtual Assistant, VA, Admin Support.
- job.language is not English.
